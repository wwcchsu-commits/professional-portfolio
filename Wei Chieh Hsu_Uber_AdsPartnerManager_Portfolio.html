02 — Rebuilding confidence in a paused account

Challenge: A streaming advertiser fully paused DSP investment after a campaign was viewed as underperforming.

Diagnosis: Identified execution delays and reporting discrepancies, including late creative submissions and mobile measurement gaps.

My contribution: Consolidated a clear post-campaign analysis, rebuilt audience proposals with sales counterparts, and aligned the customer on reporting and success criteria.

Outcome: The advertiser relaunched DSP investment, restoring an opportunity for long-term growth.

Relevance: Investigate the root cause rather than accepting the initial interpretation, rebuild trust with evidence, and follow through on execution.

EXPERIENCE & APPROACH

Playbooks that scale

Led a Japan customer-success and optimization-team collaboration model with prioritization criteria, structured reviews, office hours, and quality feedback. Coverage expanded from five pilot accounts to 40 by November 2025.

My operating loop: Define the objective → clarify ownership → document decision rules → validate against customer situations → refine through feedback.

What I would bring: Campaign review templates, documented optimization hypotheses, product adoption FAQs, and clear escalation paths developed with the team.

A playbook should improve decisions, not replace judgment. Recommendations must reflect each partner’s goals, evidence, and constraints.

EXPERIENCE & APPROACH

How I would approach partner engagement

Illustrative approach for discussion — not prior Uber delivery.

Discover: Align with the Client Partner and delivery account team on restaurant partner goals, seasonality, investment priorities, and constraints.

Diagnose: Agree on campaign KPIs, baseline trends, measurement windows, and the limits of available data.

Recommend: Prioritize tactics or format tests based on the partner’s marketing goal; distinguish observed performance from claims of incremental impact.

Execute and review: Confirm owners and timelines, monitor results, explain deviations, and recommend next actions using agreed criteria.

Scale: Turn validated learnings into reusable guidance while retaining partner-specific judgment.
