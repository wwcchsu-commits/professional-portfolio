Case 1: Designing a partner enablement and launch-readiness program

Challenge

A new advertising capability was ready for global rollout, but partners across markets needed a structured, confidence-building path to adopt it, and the product team needed credible field signal before broader launch.

Approach

  • Conducted user-acceptance testing ahead of local launch and documented workflow and product gaps.
  • Partnered with global Product Managers and GTM specialists to prioritize fixes and shape a locally relevant launch narrative.
  • Built partner-facing playbooks covering positioning, setup, operations, troubleshooting, measurement, and best practices.
  • Delivered enablement sessions tailored to each partner's maturity and business priorities, rather than a one-size-fits-all rollout.

Outcome

Created a repeatable path from product availability to confident partner adoption, and improved the quality of field feedback reaching global product and GTM teams.

Case 2: Building a cross-team operating framework from pilot to scale

Challenge

Sales and partner-facing teams needed a consistent way to identify and act on campaign-optimization and expansion opportunities, but no shared operating model existed.

Approach

  • Partnered with Sales to define a structured framework for identifying optimization and expansion opportunities.
  • Piloted the framework with a subset of partners to validate the workflow and success criteria.
  • Refined the process based on pilot results and drove it through to full implementation across the broader partner base.

Outcome

Established a shared, scalable operating rhythm between partner-facing and sales teams, improving consistency in how expansion opportunities were identified and pursued.
