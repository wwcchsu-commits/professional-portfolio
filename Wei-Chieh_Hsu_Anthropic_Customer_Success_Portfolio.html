01 · Recovering a paused strategic customer

Trust recoverySuccess planning
Situation

A major streaming and entertainment advertiser paused investment after results were perceived as inefficient, putting the relationship and future growth at risk.

Actions

Revisited business goals and measurement context, clarified performance gaps, aligned stakeholders on a staged plan, and connected recommendations to shared success measures.

Outcome: Investment grew more than 16× after the relationship and growth plan were reset. Customer identity and confidential revenue details are omitted.

02 · Scaling customer coverage without losing consistency

Operating modelCustomer engagement
Situation

Specialist capacity was limited and engagement patterns were inconsistent across accounts.

Actions

Built account-selection criteria, a semi-dynamic coverage model, office hours, monthly business reviews, quality checks, and escalation paths.

Outcome: Scaled coverage from 5 to 35+ active accounts with a repeatable engagement approach.

03 · Driving adoption of a new solution

EnablementChange adoption
Situation

Product availability alone did not guarantee adoption; teams needed a clear value narrative, onboarding, and reusable proof points.

Actions

Supported commercialization with customer examples, onboarding materials, field enablement, feedback loops, and consistent follow-up.

Outcome: Adoption expanded from 15 to 175 advertisers.

04 · Translating technical insight into customer outcomes

AnalyticsROI
Situation

Technical recommendations need to be understood in the customer’s business context before they become action.

Actions

Partnered with Data Science, translated findings into practical recommendations, aligned actions with advertiser goals, and followed through on adoption.

Outcome: Four advertisers adopted recommendations, with up to 110% ROAS improvement. An AI-assisted reporting framework separately reduced manual analysis from days to hours.

How I would support Anthropic customers in ASEAN

  • Build a shared success plan: connect customer objectives to priority workflows, stakeholders, adoption milestones, and measurable outcomes.
  • Identify underused value: review usage and customer feedback with technical partners to uncover barriers and broader adoption opportunities.
  • Support change management: create enablement, train-the-trainer, and champion mechanisms suited to each customer.
  • Coordinate the account team: bring customer context to Sales, technical specialists, and Product; make ownership and next steps explicit.
  • Capture and communicate value: use business reviews to document progress, ROI evidence, blockers, and expansion opportunities.
  • Build regional practices: use Mandarin fluency and cross-market experience to adapt communication and playbooks to regional nuances.
