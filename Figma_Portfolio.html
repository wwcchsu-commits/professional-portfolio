03 · Enablement Approach

How I turn a new tool into a kept habit

A practical loop, not a framework for its own sake — built around what actually determines whether a customer keeps using something after the first session.

01 — DISCOVER

Start with outcomes

Align stakeholders on the actual goal, workflow, and blocker — not the feature list. What does "working" look like to them?

02 — ENABLE

Build real confidence

Workshops, champions, and feedback loops calibrated to where the team actually is — not a one-size deck.

03 — PROVE

Make value visible

Track adoption against the outcome that mattered at the start. Surface blockers early, before they become renewal risk.

04 — SCALE

Expand what works

Turn a working pattern into a reusable playbook — and feed what's learned back to product and account teams.
