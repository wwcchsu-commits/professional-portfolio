The Situation

A new data-driven ad product needed to scale from closed beta to GA in Japan. Partners had no reason to trust an unproven, technically complex product. Six months post-GA, adoption remained below expectations.

The Approach

  • Recruited early adopters during closed beta; developed Japan-based case studies from live deployments
  • Built the business case grounded in performance data; delivered trainings and provided ongoing PM feedback
  • When adoption stalled, took ownership: collected case studies from production, synthesized into enablement materials
  • Mentored other consultants; delivered two-phase internal enablement program to drive broader adoption

The Outcome

Grew adoption from 5 to 35 accounts (60% rate vs. 14% global). Established a repeatable enablement model for long-term scalability.

Relevance to Sierra

Shows ownership of a full product lifecycle — from recruiting early adopters through building enablement and scaling adoption. Directly relevant to bringing new products from concept to production at scale within customer environments.
