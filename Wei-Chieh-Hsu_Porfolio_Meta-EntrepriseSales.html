Motivation

Why this role, and why Meta again

I started my career on the platform side of advertising at Meta, as a Client Solutions Manager in Taipei. Since then I have spent several years at Amazon Ads Japan on the other side of complex advertiser and agency conversations — owning a $17.5M+ portfolio and working daily with Product, Data Science, and regional leadership to turn platform capability into client results. What draws me back is the chance to take that experience into an enterprise-facing role where the job is to help senior decision-makers see how a platform solves a real business problem.

The part of this work I do best is the space between the business conversation and the technical one. When a leading streaming advertiser paused its investment, I worked out that the real problem was a measurement mismatch, not platform performance, and rebuilt the strategy around that. When agencies struggled with persistent efficiency issues, I worked directly with a cross-region Data Science team and turned their analysis into guidance traders could act on. I am increasingly doing this with AI tools as well, using them on real client work to process large agency campaign histories and surface adoption opportunities.

I want to be direct about scope: my background is advertising solutions and client strategy, not technical architecture design or enterprise systems integration. I am applying because I want to build that technical depth inside a role that rewards the client and cross-functional strengths I already have, and I would rather say that plainly than overstate it.
