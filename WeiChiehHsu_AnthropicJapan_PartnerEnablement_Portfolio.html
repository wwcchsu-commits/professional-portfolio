03 · Illustrative Japan Partner Readiness Approach

Activate global programs locally; measure demonstrated readiness

Proposed framework, not prior SI or Claude delivery. PAMs retain commercial ownership; central teams own curriculum and certification; training partners deliver sessions. Assessment design must align with the global competency framework.

01 · Align

Map roles and priorities

Work with Japan PAMs and SI practice, alliance, and L&D leaders to identify priority partners, practitioner roles, and readiness gaps.

02 · Plan

Tier coverage responsibly

Build readiness plans for 3–5 leadership-prioritized partners and scalable paths for the rest, using central competency definitions adapted to local staffing.

03 · Activate

Coordinate the existing portfolio

Work with central training teams and localized delivery partners to activate appropriate programs, support participation, and gather practical barriers.

04 · Validate

Measure capability, not attendance

Agree on role-relevant evidence of skill with central owners; track assessed readiness, application, and recurring gaps separately from completions and commercial results.
