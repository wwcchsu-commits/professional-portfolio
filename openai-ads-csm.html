Case 1: Launch readiness for a new advertising product

Challenge

A new advertising capability was ready globally, but local advertisers and agencies needed a practical path from awareness to confident use, and the product team needed real field signal before wider rollout.

Approach

  • Conducted user-acceptance testing ahead of local launch and documented workflow and product gaps.
  • Partnered with global Product Managers and GTM specialists to prioritize fixes and align launch messaging with local market needs.
  • Built partner-facing playbooks covering positioning, setup, operations, troubleshooting, and reporting.
  • Delivered enablement sessions tailored to each partner's maturity and business goals.

Outcome

Created a repeatable path from product availability to advertiser adoption, and improved the quality of field feedback reaching global product teams.

Case 2: Measurement that changes advertiser decisions

Challenge

Advertisers had access to campaign reporting but lacked a clear, decision-ready view of what was actually driving results, or what to test next.

Approach

  • Built full-funnel measurement plans connecting business goals to audience, conversion, and campaign-performance data.
  • Designed structured experiments, including A/B tests and conversion-lift studies, and translated results into a clear narrative for decision-makers.
  • Partnered with Product Managers and Data Scientists to resolve technical performance questions and turn findings into advertiser-ready guidance.
