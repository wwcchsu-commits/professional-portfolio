Case 1 — Global-to-local launch readiness

Challenge

New advertising capabilities often arrive with a global product narrative but require local-market translation. Partners need answers beyond “what does this feature do?”: when should it be used, how does it fit an existing workflow, what success signals matter, and where should an issue be escalated?

Approach

  • Conducted user-acceptance testing and documented product or workflow gaps before local launch.
  • Partnered with global Product Managers and GTM specialists to clarify readiness priorities and communicate field constraints.
  • Developed partner-facing playbooks covering positioning, planning considerations, operational steps, troubleshooting, reporting, and best practices.
  • Delivered onboarding and enablement tailored to partner maturity, business goals, and local operating realities.

Outcome

Created a more repeatable path from product availability to partner adoption, while improving the quality of feedback shared with global teams. The work strengthened alignment between product intent, commercial readiness, and customer execution.

Case 2 — Measurement that changes decisions

Challenge

Advertisers often see campaign results but lack a confident, decision-ready view of incrementality, conversion impact, or the next test worth running. The goal was to move measurement from reporting into a tool for improving investment decisions.

Approach

  • Connected business objectives to full-funnel measurement plans using audience, conversion, and campaign-performance signals.
  • Designed structured experiments, including A/B testing and conversion-lift testing, and translated outcomes into a simple narrative for decision-makers.
  • Worked with Product Managers and Data Scientists to investigate complex performance questions and turn technical findings into actionable guidance.
  • Used workshops and post-campaign reviews to raise partner measurement literacy and establish the next learning agenda.

Selected measurable outcomes

41%Reduction in media cost for a health-supplement advertiser through a structured A/B test.
44%Improvement in cost per purchase for a retail advertiser through a conversion-lift test.
