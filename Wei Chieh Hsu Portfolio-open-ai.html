Motivation

Building the ecosystem while the rules are still being written

ChatGPT Ads creates a rare opportunity to design a partner model before it becomes institutionalized — before the coverage logic, the enablement rhythms, and the governance mechanics calcify into whatever gets built first. That's the specific moment I want to work in.

My experience has taught me that scaled partner growth requires more than relationships: it requires clear coverage, repeatable workflows, credible proof points, enablement, governance, and metrics. At Amazon Ads Japan, that meant redesigning a fragmented coverage model into one that scaled from 5 pilot accounts to 35+ active ones, and building launch enablement that took three Tier-1 agencies from zero to fully trained in two weeks for a new product.

I want to be direct about scope: I am not presenting myself as someone who has already run a formal reseller program. I am presenting evidence that I have built many of the operating mechanics a reseller program needs — selection logic, scaled coverage, governance rhythms, enablement, and adoption measurement — and done so inside a complex programmatic advertising ecosystem. I want to bring that operating blueprint to OpenAI, and extend it into the reseller economics and incentive mechanics that this role will build.

01 · Ecosystem Design

From "managed agencies" to designed ecosystem mechanics

The strongest overlap with this OpenAI role is not simply agency relationship management. It is the operating architecture I have built around partner selection, scaled coverage, enablement, governance, adoption measurement, and product feedback.

Selection & segmentation logic

Defined revenue-prioritized account selection and pilot criteria to focus limited specialist capacity on the highest-impact opportunities.

Scaled coverage model

Built a flexible support model using confirmed and pre-approved backup accounts, reducing gatekeeping and allowing partner support to scale.

Governance rhythms

Established cross-team syncs, monthly business reviews, office hours, quality reviews, and escalation loops to improve consistency and accountability.

Partner enablement

Designed role-specific onboarding, playbooks, launch training, and capability-building programs tied to real advertiser use cases.

Adoption measurement

Tracked recommendation adoption, campaign outcomes, product adoption, and revenue signals to determine where programs should expand or improve.

Voice of ecosystem

Consolidated agency questions and product feedback and built durable working mechanisms with Product and Data Science teams.
