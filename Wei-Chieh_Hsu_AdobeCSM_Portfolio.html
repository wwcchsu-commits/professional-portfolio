03 · Customer Success Approach

How I turn adoption into a repeatable Customer Success motion

A practical loop aligned with Adobe's CSP model: understand the real customer objective, design the right enablement, inspect adoption and value, and update the plan based on what the evidence says.

01 — DIAGNOSE

Start with the customer's workflow

Understand the business objective, process, stakeholder map, adoption level, and blocker before recommending a feature or use case.

02 — ENABLE

Design the right intervention

Choose the right audience, workshop, champion model, content, and timing so enablement changes behavior rather than becoming another presentation.

03 — GOVERN

Make value visible

Track adoption and business outcomes, surface disengagement or risk early, and use evidence to decide what the account needs next.

04 — UPDATE & SCALE

Keep the success plan alive

Feed learning back into the plan, turn successful use cases into reusable playbooks, and expand what works across more teams and workflows.
