03 · Customer Success Approach

How I turn a merchant objective into a growth plan

A practical loop aligned with the role's mix of long-term strategy and day-to-day execution.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Start with the merchant's goal

Clarify business priorities, stakeholder expectations, economics, customer experience, and what success needs to look like.

02 — DIAGNOSE

Find the real growth lever

Use product, performance, customer, and operational data to identify the constraint or opportunity that matters most.

03 — ALIGN & EXECUTE

Build a plan people can act on

Turn analysis into a compelling narrative, align internal and external stakeholders, and drive the required product or operating actions.

04 — MEASURE & SCALE

Make the outcome visible

Inspect results, update the account strategy, surface new opportunities, and scale successful patterns across more customers or use cases.
