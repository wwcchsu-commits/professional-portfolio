03 · Program Approach

How I design a partner program that outlives me

A practical loop for building programs from zero — not a framework for its own sake, but the sequence that determines whether a program becomes a durable playbook or a one-off effort.

1. Design

Define who the partner is, what they own, and what "good" looks like — before building any material or process around it.

2. Enable

Build onboarding and certification matched to where each partner actually is — not a single deck for every partner type.

3. Govern

Track partner health against real outcomes. Surface risk early, before it becomes an escalation or a lost relationship.

4. Scale

Turn what worked into a reusable framework — documented well enough that someone else can run it without me in the room.
