01 · Why Datadog

Why I want to move deeper into enterprise technology Customer Success

The role sits at the intersection of strategic customer ownership, technical understanding, adoption, and long-term value realization — the direction my work has increasingly moved toward.

My career began in media strategy, but the work I have found most meaningful has increasingly been platform-side: understanding why a customer is not realizing value, bringing the right technical and product expertise into the conversation, and translating complex platform capability into a clear success plan.

At Amazon Ads, that has meant business reviews, adoption programs, measurement diagnostics, product launches, Data Science collaboration, and scalable support mechanisms. I am now looking to apply that Customer Success discipline to a broader enterprise technology platform where adoption, usage maturity, and long-term customer value are at the center of the relationship.

Datadog is compelling because its Customer Success model combines exactly those elements: onboarding through maturity, success planning, customer health, technical-business alignment, and a strong culture of learning. I would bring deep Japan-market experience and enterprise stakeholder management while deliberately building deeper expertise in observability and security.

04 · Customer Success Framework

How I would run a strategic enterprise account from onboarding to maturity

A practical loop aligned to Datadog's success-plan, adoption, customer-health, and technical-business alignment model.

01 — DISCOVER

Define success and stakeholder priorities

Understand the business and technical objectives, pain points, decision-makers, adoption stage, and what meaningful value needs to look like.

02 — PLAN & ADOPT

Build the success plan and operating cadence

Translate discovery into prioritized outcomes, onboarding or adoption actions, owners, milestones, business reviews, and the right technical engagement.

03 — INSPECT HEALTH

Use evidence to find risk and opportunity

Track usage and outcome signals, surface friction early, diagnose the real blocker, and align stakeholders on the intervention that matters most.

04 — REALIZE & SCALE

Make value visible and reusable

Connect adoption back to business impact, identify the next growth opportunity, and turn successful patterns into best practices for the broader team.
