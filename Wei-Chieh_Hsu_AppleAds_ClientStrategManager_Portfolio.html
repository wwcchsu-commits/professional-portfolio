03 · Management Approach

How I would raise a Growth Accounts team's success rate

The common thread across these cases is a practical leadership loop: focus the team on the right opportunities, develop judgment through real work, inspect the business with data, and institutionalize what works.

01 — PRIORITIZE

Make the portfolio strategy explicit

Clarify where growth exists, where risk sits, which clients require leadership attention, and where team time creates the most leverage.

02 — DEVELOP

Coach judgment in context

Develop discovery, analytical thinking, performance fluency, product depth, and commercial discipline through real account work and structured feedback.

03 — INSPECT

Turn data into a team decision

Use pipeline, account, performance, adoption, and customer signals to identify gaps early and focus coaching or resources where they matter most.

04 — SCALE

Make strong behavior reusable

Turn successful account motions into playbooks, operating cadence, onboarding, review mechanisms, and examples that others can run without me in the room.
